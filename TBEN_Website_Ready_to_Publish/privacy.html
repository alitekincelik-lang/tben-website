Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

TBEN is currently being developed as an independent professional business network. This policy explains how information submitted through this website is handled.

Information we collect

When you register your interest or contact us, we may receive your name, company, email address, location, business profile and any message you provide.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, keep you informed about TBEN's development and future membership opportunities, and administer legitimate network communications.

Legal basis and retention

We rely on your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to your request. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes or to meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers used to host the website or manage communications, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing info@tben.uk.
